Orbita

One tap. Endless orbit. Free to play, works offline.

Orbita is a minimal one-touch arcade game. Your ball circles a planet. Tap to launch it into the next orbit. Land it, or drift into the void. Red arcs spin around every planet and they do not forgive a late tap.

How to play

  • Tap anywhere to leave your current orbit.
  • You always fly along the direction you were spinning, so timing is everything.
  • Release exactly towards the next planet for a PERFECT landing — worth double points.
  • Touch a red arc and the run is over.
